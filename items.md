The following types of items exist:

* Books - give Redraw Selected
* Navigation Tools - give Redraw All
* Light Sources - give bonus drawn tokens
* Weapons - give Resolve
* Jewelry - give Insight
* Food - give Stamina when picked up, maximum Stamina, and a small amount of bonus Stamina after every encounter

The player can hold 3 items at a time. Any 3 items can be used, with the exception of Light Sources (see below)

Bonus token draws are very carefully controlled. Only Light Sources can ever have bonus token draws, and only one Light Source can ever be equipped at a time. Light sources with +1, +2, and +3 token draws begin appearing on levels 6, 12, and 18, respectively. (The intent is that the player should usually get exactly one Light Source that maxes them out for draws, as soon as they are able.)

The item shop follows the following rules for stocking:
* The shop only offers items that the player can afford.
* If the player can't afford even the best normal items, it offers special "weak" items that cost only $1 and offer 1 Insight, 1 Resolve, or stamina regen _without_ the stamina maximum.
* At and beyond level 6, the shop will always offer at least one Light Source of the highest token draw that the player can afford.
* If the player is missing 25% or more of their HP, shops will always have at least one Food item
* All of these other rules taken into account, the game should fill in the rest of the slots by choosing a number of random items equal to twice the free slots, and choosing the most expensive ones that the player can afford. This should bias the selection to expensive items, but cheaper ones can sometimes still be found.

Shops contain 3 items to choose from.

Baseline costs are as follows (may change, but the structure should be recorded here):
Redraw Selected: $4
Redraw All: $2
Bonus Token Draw: $5 (cheap, because if they player misses it they will be hurting bad)
Resolve: $3 for 2 Resolve
Insight: same
Stamina: $3 for 10 max Stamina, 10 Stamina when picked up, and +2 Stamina after every encounter.

Contrary to a previous version, these costs are flat and do not scale. For instance, an Book that provides +1 Redraw Selected and 2 Resolve costs $4 + $3 = $7.

The names of items should be static, not randomly generated.
