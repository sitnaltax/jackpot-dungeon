The following types of items exist:

* Books - give Redraw Selected
* Navigation Tools - give Redraw All
* Light Sources - give bonus drawn tokens
* Weapons - give Resolve
* Jewelry - give Insight
* Food - give Stamina when picked up, maximum Stamina, and a small amount of bonus Stamina after every encounter

The player can hold 3 items at a time. Any 3 items can be used, with the exception of Light Sources (see below)

Bonus token draws are very carefully controlled. Only Light Sources can ever have bonus token draws, and only one Light Source can ever be equipped at a time. Furthermore, +2 token draw Light Sources will never appear before depth 14. (The intent is that the player should usually get exactly one Light Source that maxes them out for draws, as soon as they are able.)

* If the player is missing 25% or more of their HP, shops will always have at least one Food item
* At depth 8 and beyond, shops always carry at least one Light Source. At and beyond depth 14, they always carry one with +2 token draws. They may carry others, but are guaranteed to have at least one.

Shops contain 3 items to choose from.

Baseline costs are as follows (may change, but the structure should be recorded here):
Redraw Selected: 3 XP
Redraw All: 2 XP
Bonus Token Draw: 5 XP (cheap, because if they player misses it they will be hurting bad)
Resolve: 3 XP for 2 Resolve
Insight: same
Stamina: 3 XP for 10 max Stamina, 10 Stamina when picked up, and +2 Stamina after every encounter.

In addition, every level of the resource beyond the first costs +1 XP. Items can provide two (or more) resources, but each additional resource also costs +1 XP. As an example, one Navigation Tool, the Silver Sextant, might give +1 Redraw All and also 2 Resolve. It would cost 2 XP for the base Redraw All, +3 for the Resolve, +1 for a secondary resource, = 6 total.


The player should get roughly 2-2.5 XP per battle,  scaling up to around 4 by midgame. They should be able to buy something at most, but not all, shops.

The names of items should be static, not randomly generated.
